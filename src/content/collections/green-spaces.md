A collection of greenware (unfired clay) showcasing Nottingham city centre's green spaces. These were all made when I wanted others to realise the importance of these spaces for our mental health and for the climate. The maps were made by taking a flat piece of clay, cut out into the shape of the green space - or a walk that went through it, to the green space to imprint with the textures that you could find there. I want others looking at these pieces to be reminded of their own local park and how much it has given them over the years: talks with friends, walking their dogs, playing with their kids, absorbing the sun - all encased in trees, flowers, shrubs, and grasses.
