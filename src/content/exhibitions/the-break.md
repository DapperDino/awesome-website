The Break at the Twitchel Gallery (Nottingham Trent University) 2022

What have you been making during the break? The exhibition in the Twitchel Gallery, was on the theme ‘The Break’, running from 19th – 25th October.

Exhibition featuring:

** *Redaction* by Viola Van Dulleman

** *Delicacy* by Sophie Lynn-Carney

** *Summer in the Greenhouse* by Katie Rayson

** *Geoffery Make a Run For It* by Liza Gooden

**Viola Van Dulleman**

Viola has gathered and collected souvenirs from all over the world, and yet nothing has made her feel closer to her home than when she’s created her own mementos. Over her time at NTU she’s developed a great appreciation for casting and film photography, and she enjoys the intimacy and rawness that these art forms bring. ‘Redaction’ and ‘Tongue’ both explore her connection with Hong Kong, and display the different time periods she recalls when looking back at her time there. With the political unrest currently going on, she feels an urge to protect the place she loves, and that has in the past protected her. This week, she invites you to walk through her memories with her.

**Sophie Lynn-Carney**

In her recent practice Sophie has been exploring emotions of isolation and vulnerability through using a mix of artwork and text. She is interested in emotionally driven and atmospheric art. Her artwork consists mainly of drawings and sculptures. She likes to create key images / motifs in her practice and place them into a new significance through text – both written by her and deliberately appropriated. By placing these together, she feels it gives it a narrative quality. In her 3D practice she finds it interesting to explore the personification of objects and finds it effective to demonstrate ideas of fragile emotions. When using objects in her work she hopes to make the audience empathise from a place of distance – anyone being able to put their own emotions onto the object. She finds atmosphere to be incredibly important to her work – she tries hard to keep a quiet and isolated atmosphere with all her pieces. Her drawings are light and faded, drawn without backgrounds to give clear direction and isolation to the key image depicted. Her 3D pieces are isolated and fragile, and in colours she finds express fragility.

**Katie Rayson**

Katie’s artwork gives people the opportunity to reflect and converse about the climate crisis. Artists of all types can keep the conversation on the topic fresh and hopefully inspire the public to engage with climate crisis solution-based industries. The nature-based motifs and concepts calm the audience. It gives you a sense of space to let go of worries, especially climate anxiety, and responsibility that the world throws at you. Summer in the Greenhouse is a continuation of Katie’s practice looking at the garden as a safe space to relax and reconnect with nature.

**Liza Gooden**

In her third year of a BA in Fine Art at NTU, Gooden has come to the frightful understanding, she is a hoarder! Her practice embraces foraging, found objects and bricolage along with the desire to have a little fun. With an environmental viewpoint and the Artist’s Joke central, she makes with her hands to create sculptural pieces, inviting the viewer to reflect on recycling waste, ponder and maybe chuckle. Geoffrey Make a Run for it – 2022, reflects how she feels sometimes, juxtaposed with the reality of a dog who ate through his lead, and she could not throw it away.
