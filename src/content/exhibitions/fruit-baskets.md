**Exhibition Information**

An exhibition by Connection Collective at Fishergate Point Studios. Opening Saturday 28th October 2023, and ran until Saturday 11th November 2023. 

Connection Collective are a group of East Midlands Fine Art Graduates who connect through artwork based on environmentalism, LGBTQ+ rights, mental health, disability activism and storytelling resident at BACKLIT studio. 

In their inaugural exhibition at Fishergate Point Studios, The Fruit Baskets we see how members form connections with one another’s individual practices through collaboration. Full of silliness and queer diaspora we celebrate all the fruity members and Nottingham’s LGBTQ+ history. 

This exhibition is in collaboration with Seraphic Sounds. 
Optional ticket booking profits for launch night went to the charity Mermaids UK, a trans and non-binary youth support centre.
