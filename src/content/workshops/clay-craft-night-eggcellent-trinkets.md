Come along for a cozy evening of crafting with clay! Artist led - step by step you'll create an Easter bunny and a mother hen!

During your craft night at the Malt Cross, Katie (your clay expert) will get your creative energy flowing with a game of clay charades. Once the winning team has been decided, you will be guided step by step to create adorable Easter trinkets - an Easter bunny and a mother hen with her chicks!

At the end of the evening you will head home with a spring in your step, and your new creations in a neat little box.
