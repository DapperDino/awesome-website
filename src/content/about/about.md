Facilitator & artist driven by curiosity, well being, and the outdoors. A colleague with proven communication & organisational skills.

She has collaborated on, managed, & delivered multiple events including events for Connecting Notts, Connection Collective, Nonsuch Studios, Nottingham Contemporary, and Backlit. 

Constantly developing her skill set, Katie is always on the look out for experiences that will help her get outside her comfort zone.
