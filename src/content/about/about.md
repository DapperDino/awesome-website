**Artist Statement**

I sculpt clay texture maps. I am passionate about places, no matter how small, that make an impact on the climate crisis. All the maps are carefully imprinted with textures found on site, from litter to leaves. With no restrictions on what is imprinted, we see our impact and the beauty of the location. The clay maps are not fired in a kiln, to reduce the carbon footprint. As a result, the sculptures are as fragile as the current climate. I want you to feel gratitude, love, & duty for your local green space & our planet.

**Workshop Facilitating**

Katie is passionate about working with young people: facilitating workshops for families with Ashfield Creates & Connecting Notts as well as 5+ years planning & co-leading guide sessions as a Girl Guiding Leader. 

She has collaborated on, managed, & delivered multiple events including events for Connecting Notts, Connection Collective, Nonsuch Studios, Nottingham Contemporary, and Backlit. 

Constantly developing her skill set, Katie is always on the look out for experiences that will help her get outside her comfort zone.
